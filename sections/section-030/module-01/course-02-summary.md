# Summary

Threats can exploit vulnerabilities to harm assets. Risk relates the possible harm to its likelihood and context, while controls aim to reduce that risk. Prevention, detection and correction have different purposes.

Frameworks organize control goals and evidence; benchmarks inform configuration. Effective assessment checks deployed behavior, ownership and residual risk. A policy or checklist alone cannot establish that the intended protection is operating.
