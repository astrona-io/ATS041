# Summary

Artifact identity, trusted origin, component inventory and vulnerability findings provide different evidence. Their value depends on verifying the exact content selected for deployment and protecting the identities involved in building and publication.

Repositories, credentials and runtime permissions form additional boundaries. A signed or scanned image still needs appropriate privilege restrictions, maintained dependencies and application security. Verification results must remain traceable to the deployed artifact rather than only to a familiar tag.
