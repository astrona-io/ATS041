# Summary

Cloud, cluster, container and code security cover related assets and responsibilities. Each layer relies on the systems below it while adding controls over its own behavior. Confidentiality, integrity and availability describe different protection goals.

Defense in depth combines relevant preventive, detective and corrective controls. A complete explanation identifies the access path, responsible component, residual risk and recovery mechanism rather than assuming one successful check guarantees every layer is secure.
